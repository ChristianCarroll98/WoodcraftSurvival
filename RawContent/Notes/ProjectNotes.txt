ORM Texture note:
In Unreal Engine 5.8, ORM textures must have sRGB disabled and compression set to "Masks" to ensure Occlusion, Roughness, and Metalness channels are treated as linear math data rather than color, preventing visual distortions [1]. For C++ projects, this is achieved by setting SRGB = false and CompressionSettings = TC_Masks on the UTexture2D object to avoid improper gamma correction on packed data [1]. You can read more about texture optimization in Unreal Engine's official documentation. [1, 2, 3]

[2] [https://www.youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GgZRO4Uxso)

Note on animating fibrous stem stripping: Spline IK with Hook ModifiersThis method uses a Blender Curve to guide the bones. By hooking the curve's control points to empty objects, you can animate them strictly when the tension reaches them.Step 1: Create the Curve and ArmatureAdd a Curve: In Object Mode, add a Bezier Curve or Path alongside your plant stem.Align the Bones: Build your bone chain so it perfectly matches the length and shape of the curve.Apply Spline IK: Go to Pose Mode, select the last bone in the chain, and add a Spline IK constraint.Set the Target to your Curve.Set the Chain Length to the total number of bones in your fiber strip.The bones will now strictly follow the shape of the curve.Step 2: Add Hook Modifiers to Control TensionTo make bones "wait" until they are pulled, you control the curve's vertices using Empty objects:Select the Curve and enter Edit Mode.Select the vertex at the very tip of the fiber. Press Ctrl + H and choose Hook to New Object. This creates an Empty that controls just that tip.Repeat this process for every single vertex down the curve, creating a sequence of Empties.Step 3: Animate the "Delayed Pull" (Peeling Effect)Now, instead of moving the bones or the curve directly, you animate the Empties in a staggered sequence:Frame 1: Keyframe all the Empties resting flat against the plant stem.Frame 10: Grab the tip Empty and pull it away. The first segment of the fiber stretches and peels.Frame 15: Once the tip Empty travels far enough to pull the fiber taut, begin animating the second Empty to follow it.Frame 20: Begin animating the third Empty, and so on.Because each vertex stays completely still until you manually animate its corresponding Empty, the lower parts of the plant fiber will remain perfectly glued to the stem until the rip sequence reaches them.

Architecture reminder:
- Fragments = data + minimal spawn/init hooks. No runtime work in fragments.
- Actors stay thin. Behaviour lives in systems.
- Create / destroy item Instances only through UItemFactorySubsystem.
- StrikeMode is orientation / incidence, not damage type. None = no orient (blunt). Stone is SingleEdged + Blunt primitives. T1 set: ItemSystemImplementation §3.
- Dual-mesh tools: Secondary WeldTo Primary. No Physics Constraint. Material overrides on the Definition for assembled ORM.
- Physical Materials = density / mass + future impact. Named primitives choose candidate damage type.
- Content assets: DA_Type_Name (DA_Item_Stone, DA_Harvestable_Nettle, DA_Recipe_SharpenedStone, DA_Minigame_Grind, DA_Structure_Workbench). Recipes sit next to the primary output item. Minigames live in Content/WoodcraftSurvival/Crafting/Minigames/.
- Crafting: UCraftingComponent lives in Player/ next to HeldItems. Recipe / minigame / movement data stay under Crafting/. Snapshot rebuilds from HeldItems OnHeldItemsChanged while idle. Session freezes snapshot updates. IMC swap for the session. Ground camera transform on the component. Engage = tool hand or default Right. CompleteCraft: durability, DestroyHeldItem on consumed, Factory spawn, auto-equip one, pile rest. Factory DestroyItemActorAndInstance is the only item destroy path. UCraftMovement modules are data. FindMove<T>(Phase) is the current stage. Minigame owns TArray<FCraftStage>; Grind / Twist / Strip are one stage, Tie is three. Local 0–1 per stage. bProgressDrivesMontage seeks the FPArms TwoHanded clip from Progress after IntroDone. Presentations spawn at AnimRef / ArmsPivot and sample Progress after intro. Enums in WoodcraftTypes.h.
- Debug flags in WoodcraftTypes.h: GbDebugHits, GbDebugSwing, GbDebugCraft, GbDebugHarvest. One bool per system gates prints and draws.
