notes on ORM textures:
Step 1: Fix the Texture Settings (Crucial)Import your ORM texture into Unreal Engine.Double-click the texture to open its settings.In the right panel, uncheck the box for SRGB.Change the Compression Settings dropdown to Masks (no sRGB).If you skip this, Unreal applies gamma correction to your math channels, making your roughness channel way darker (shinier) than it actually is.
